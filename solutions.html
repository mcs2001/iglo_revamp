Solutions

Comprehensive, temperature-controlled cold chain logistics solutions engineered to protect product integrity from origin to destination across Malaysia and Southeast Asia.

Multi-Temperature Storage & Warehousing

State-of-the-art cold storage facilities situated in Port Klang[cite: 1], operating seamlessly from chilled environments down to -25°C. Designed to accommodate food, beverage, and pharmaceutical products with rigorous inventory control[cite: 4, 6].

  • Automated Warehouse Management Systems (WMS) for real-time tracking
  • Strict FEFO and FIFO batch rotation protocols
  • JAKIM Halal, HACCP, and ISO 22000 certified standards

Refrigerated Transportation & Distribution

A dependable, GPS-monitored fleet of refrigerated trucks providing seamless distribution across Peninsular Malaysia, backed by reliable cross-border capabilities into Singapore and Thailand.

  • Continuous temperature loggers and real-time GPS tracking
  • Maintained thermal chain integrity from loading dock to delivery point
  • Experienced drivers trained in cold chain handling guidelines

Blast Freezing & Value-Added Services

Specialized processing support including high-capacity blast freezing for rapid core temperature pull-down, controlled thawing rooms, and full container plug-in and handling services.

  • Rapid blast freezing tunnels for maximum freshness retention
  • Controlled thawing chambers designed to minimize drip loss
  • Custom repacking, labeling, and container clearance support
